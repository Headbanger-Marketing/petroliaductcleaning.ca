FAQs

Before You Call, Check Here

If your question is not here, it is still worth a quick call.

How often should an older Petrolia home be cleaned?

Heritage homes here do well at every two to three years, since retrofit ductwork holds dust differently than new builds and the street trees add their own contribution each spring. Homes with ongoing renovations go more often.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Certified and insured, every job, with the paperwork to show it. Ontario issues no trade licence for duct cleaning, so certification, training, and insurance carry the weight, and all three are in place with us.

Can you prove the cleaning in a house this old?

Yes, and gently. Camera photos of the trunk lines before and after, taken with equipment sized for old metal, reviewed with you before we leave. The house gets shown, not strained.

Our dryer takes two cycles. Is it worth a service call?

Almost always. Slow drying with warm air is the classic restricted-vent signature, and the winding basement runs common here clog exactly that way. We clear the full path and measure the flow.

What does a whole-home cleaning involve, and how long?

Negative-pressure vacuum at the trunk lines, registers off and washed, each run brushed under suction, blower compartment cleaned, photos at the start and end. Most homes take three to four hours; large heritage houses with additions take more.

The vents carry an old-house smell. Can that change?

Genuinely, yes. The cleaning removes the residue holding the odour, and the sanitizer applied to the clean duct walls addresses the rest. Homes that have carried a smell for decades air out in a day.

Why does the dust return so fast after cleaning?

Almost always suction-side leaks, with the system inhaling foundation-space air and redistributing it, a near constant in retrofit ductwork. We can test for it and seal what the test finds.

Do you work beyond Petrolia?

Yes, across the neighbouring Lambton communities including Oil Springs, Wyoming, Brigden, Arkona, Forest, and into Sarnia.

Do you serve areas outside of Petrolia, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Petrolia and nearby communities including Oil Springs, Wyoming, Brigden, Forest, and the surrounding area.
